# Scrumfolks TMS — Product Guide

**For:** Scrumfolks leadership and team  
**What this is:** A plain-English tour of the live Task Management System (CRM + TMS).  
**How to use it:** Share this as-is with the client. It describes what the product does today, who can use each part, and how daily work is meant to flow.

---

## In one sentence

Scrumfolks TMS is the office system for client brands, tasks, files, reviews, team chat, attendance, leave, performance, and billing — with the right information shown to the right role.

---

## Who uses it

| Role | What they are here to do |
|------|--------------------------|
| **Owner** | Run the agency. Full access: people, brands, tasks, money, attendance reports, leave approvals. |
| **Manager** | Plan and assign work, review files, chat on tasks, move brand workflow, see how the team is performing. Managers do **not** see rupee prices. |
| **Team** | Clock in, work assigned tasks, upload files, chat in Updates, request leave, see their own performance. |
| **HR** | Team directory, leave approvals, attendance reports, performance overview. Read-only on brands. |
| **Accounts** | Billing and prices. Read-only on brands. No team management. |

Everyone signs in with **email and password** (no one-click role buttons on the login screen). Morning (light) and Night (dark) modes are available.

---

## How a typical job moves through the system

1. **Owner or Manager creates a brand** (the client) and fills in identity: logo, fonts, colours, voice, goals, journey, and brand files.
2. **People are allocated** to that brand — managers and team members who should see the client’s documents.
3. **A task (or project) is created**, assigned to one or more people, with due date, priority, brief, optional sub-tasks, and optional Google Drive folder.
4. **The assignee gets a notification** (and a structured email brief when email delivery is switched on).
5. **The person clocks in**, then updates status, uploads work, and chats in **Updates**.
6. **If review is required**, the manager approves or asks for changes, with comments and version history.
7. **Files sit in Review** until Owner/Manager approve or reject them.
8. **When the task is completed**, the Updates channel can be closed and chat history purged. Files remain for the record.
9. **If the work is billable**, Owner or Accounts set the price and mark it billed.

Team members must **clock in** before they change task status, upload work, or review. Clock-out after a long day asks for confirmation (standard working day is treated as 9 hours).

## What’s on the left menu

### Dashboard
A home screen that matches the person’s role.

- Important and overdue work, recent activity, announcements.
- Notifications (task assigned, chat messages, flags).
- Clock in / clock out and hours worked today (on the top bar as well).

Owners and managers see the agency picture. Team members see **their** work only.

### Calendar
A month view of due dates, leave, and company events.

- Owner / Manager / HR can look at the whole company or one person.
- Everyone else sees their own calendar.

### Tasks
Create and manage work.

- Title, brief, brand (or a new project name if there is no brand), type, priority, due date, assignees.
- Optional sub-tasks with their own people and dates.
- Recurring tasks, “needs review”, and billable flag.
- **Google Drive / file links** can be added when the task is created so the folder is part of the brief.
- Status colours: Not Started, In Progress, Under Review, Revision Needed, Completed, On Hold, Struggling, Needs Attention.
- Priority colours: Critical, High, Medium, Low.
- **Assigned by** is stored and shown, so it is always clear who gave the work.
- Opening a task shows Overview, Sub-tasks, Files & links, Review (if required), and History.
- Team members only change progress on work assigned to them. Owners/Managers edit the full task. Only Owner deletes.

If someone picks **No brand**, the system asks for a project name and creates that project automatically.

### Updates (task chat)
One chat channel per task — like a focused Slack thread, not a general inbox.

- Left: list of task chats, last message, status, assignee count.
- Right: conversation for the selected task.
- Header is stacked so it stays readable: title and status, then status change, then review, then Drive, then messages, then Send.
- Paste a Drive link in chat and it becomes clickable. Folders can also be attached from **Add Drive**.
- Owner/Manager can **Approve / Reject** when the task needs review, and **Close & purge** when the job is done.
- Completing a task as Owner/Manager automatically closes the channel.

This is **task messaging**, not private one-to-one DMs.

### Workflow
A board of brand / project stages (Assigned → Design → Content → Editing → Approval → Delivered) so leadership can see where each client sits in the pipeline.

### Brands
Client records.

- List of clients with logo (or initials) and stage.
- Overview, Projects, Tasks, Goals, Identity, Journey.
- Identity: logo upload (**replaces** the existing logo, does not duplicate it), fonts, logo variants, brand colours, photography style, brand voice.
- Brand files and documents (guidelines, briefs, assets) — the logo itself is kept on the brand, not mixed into the document list.
- Allocate managers (Owner) and team (Owner/Manager). Allocated people can open that brand’s documents.
- Team members only see brands they are allocated to. HR and Accounts see brands as view-only.

Long brand names stay inside the cards instead of spilling out of the layout.

### Review
Queue of **uploaded files** for Owner and Manager.

- Filter pending / approved / rejected.
- Open the file in the in-app viewer.
- Approve or reject with comments.

Task-level review (approve the job, not just a file) lives on the task page and in Updates.

### Team
People and departments.

- Owner/Manager add team members with role, department, designation, and managers.
- A person can report to **more than one manager**.
- Owner can change a user’s email, reset a password, deactivate, or delete.
- Workload view: how loaded each person is.
- Owner/Manager can open a member and see that person’s tasks with status.

### Performance
How work is going — not a spreadsheet dump.

- **Team members** see their own numbers: allocated, delayed, completed, completed on time.
- **Owner / Manager / HR** see the team, month-wise splits, and can drill into one person.

### Attendance
Clock in and hours.

- Everyone clocks in from the top bar to start work.
- **Team** sees their own log.
- **Owner / HR / Manager** see a **team report** (not the owner’s personal punch log). In time, out time, and hours for the people they should see.

### Leave
Request and approve time off.

- Staff request leave with type, dates, and reason. Remaining balance is shown.
- Owner and HR approve or reject (rejection needs a reason).
- Leave appears on the calendar.

### Announcements
Company notices.

- Owner/Manager post with priority: Normal, Important, Urgent.
- Optional date, image, and link.
- Everyone can read them; they also surface on the dashboard.

### Billing
Money on billable tasks.

- **Only Owner and Accounts** see and set rupee prices, and mark work billed.
- Managers see that work is billable / unpriced, not the amount.
- Team does not see billing.

## Files, Drive, and viewing work

| Need | Where it lives |
|------|----------------|
| Upload a deliverable, PDF, image, zip, etc. | Task → **Files & links**, or Brand → **Brand files** |
| Keep working files in Google Drive / Dropbox / Figma | Task Drive links (create form, task page, or Updates) |
| Look at a file without downloading | In-app viewer (click the file) |
| Sign off a file | **Review** tab |
| Sign off the whole task | Task **Review** tab or Updates review bar |

Uploads accept everyday office files, up to **100 MB** each.

## Emails

When a task is created or someone new is assigned, the system can send a **formatted task brief**:

- Task title, brand, type, priority, dates  
- Who assigned it and who is on it  
- The written brief, sub-tasks, checklist  
- **Google Drive buttons** (if a folder was attached)  
- Buttons to open the task and the Updates chat  

Daily wrap-up emails can also be scheduled (pending work, overdue, due this week).

Delivery uses the agency Gmail (or another mail provider) once the mail password is configured on the server. Until that is set, briefs are prepared in the app but will not land in inboxes.

## What is in this version vs later

**In this version**

- Roles and login  
- Brands with full identity kit and documents  
- Tasks, projects, sub-tasks, Drive links, file upload and viewer  
- Updates chat per task, close when done  
- File review queue and task review with comments / versions  
- Clock-in required before work  
- Leave, announcements, calendar  
- Performance for self and for managers  
- Billing with prices only for Owner and Accounts  
- Morning / Night theme  
- Multi-manager reporting lines  
- Owner can change email, reset password, deactivate or delete a user  

**Not in this version (planned later, not missing bugs)**

- Private one-to-one chat between two people (outside a task)  
- “Forgot password” by email OTP (Owner/HR reset a password instead)  
- Exporting performance as PDF/CSV  
- Automatic old-file trash / virus scanning as a full lifecycle  
- Rich text editor (briefs are plain text today)

## Suggested walkthrough (15 minutes)

1. Sign in as **Owner**. Toggle Morning / Night.  
2. Open **Brands** → pick a client → show logo, identity, allocated people, documents. Replace the logo (it updates in place).  
3. **Tasks** → create a task, assign people, paste a Drive folder, set due date. Note **Assigned by**.  
4. Open the task page → Files, sub-tasks, Review.  
5. **Updates** → same task chat. Send a message. Show Drive on the task, then Send.  
6. Clock in → change status to Under Review → Approve or Reject with a comment.  
7. **Review** → pending files.  
8. **Attendance** → team report (not personal). **Leave** → pending requests. **Performance** → one team member.  
9. **Billing** as Owner → set a price. Log in as Manager and confirm prices are hidden.  
10. Complete the task → channel closes. Chat can be purged; files stay.

## Support notes for the client

- The live app is reached from the Scrumfolks login URL provided separately.  
- Each person gets their own email login. Passwords are set by Owner/HR, not guessed from a shared sheet.  
- Work is organised around **brands** and **tasks**. If it is not a client brand yet, create a named project from the task form.  
- Chat belongs to the **task**. Use Updates for that job; use announcements for the whole company.

This is the product as built for Scrumfolks TMS today.
